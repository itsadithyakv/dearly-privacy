# Dearly: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 6 November 2026*

Everything you write stays on your PC. We collect nothing.

## What Dearly stores

Your entries, moods, photos, voice notes and settings are stored **only on your PC**, encrypted
with AES-256. They're protected by your password, PIN or Windows Hello if you set one, and by
Windows for your account if you don't.

## What Dearly sends anywhere

**Nothing of yours.** Dearly has no account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no crash
reporting, and no artificial intelligence. We (PaperKite) never receive your journal or any
information about how you use the app.

The only times Dearly uses the internet:

- **Microsoft Store:** to show the price of Dearly Pro and process its purchase. This is handled
  by Microsoft under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
  Dearly receives only whether Pro is owned.
- **Sync (optional, off by default):** if you turn it on, Dearly writes **encrypted** files into a
  folder you choose (for example your OneDrive folder). Your own cloud app moves those files; Dearly
  never contacts any server itself. The files can't be read without your password or recovery key.
  (A PIN only works on the PC and Windows account where you set it up, so it can't be guessed from these files.)
- **Backups to a folder you choose (optional):** the same: encrypted files, in a folder you pick.

## Exports

When you export (TXT, Markdown, PDF, DOCX, HTML, JSON), Dearly creates readable copies in the folder
you choose. Keep them somewhere private.

## Children

Dearly doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices

You can export everything you wrote at any time, for free. Uninstalling Dearly removes the journal
from the app's folder; your automatic backups stay in your "Dearly Backups" folder until you delete them.

## Contact

PaperKite. Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/itsadithyakv/dearly-privacy/issues
(please don't include anything from your journal).
